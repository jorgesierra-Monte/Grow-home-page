Why filter by California rather than a city like LA?

Providers are licensed by state, not city. Filtering by state ensures you see every licensed provider who can legally treat you — whether they're based in LA, San Francisco, or anywhere in California.

Will providers take my insurance

Most of our providers accept major insurance plans. You can filter by your specific insurance in the search to only see providers who accept your plan.

Are there therapist who focus on specific issues?

Yes. Our providers specialize in everything from anxiety and depression to relationship issues, trauma, ADHD, and grief. Use the Needs filter to match with someone who has experience in what you're dealing with.

What happens during the first therapy session?

Your first session is typically an intake where you and your therapist get to know each other. You'll talk about what brought you to therapy, your goals, and how the therapist can help. It's low-pressure and conversational.
